💜 Lilac

Your UCL week, made lighter.
Timetables, deadlines, money and clubs live in a dozen places. Lilac pulls them into one joyful dashboard, and Claude turns your stress into a plan.
🎙️

Talk it out

Ramble about what's on your plate. Claude turns it into prioritised tasks with due dates and time estimates.

✨

AI week planner

Study blocks placed around lectures and meetings, weighted by deadline, with breaks and free evenings protected.

📅

Syncs your calendars

UCL timetable, Moodle deadlines and Outlook, by pasting a link or uploading an .ics file.

💷

Budget buddy

Drop in a Revolut or bank CSV. Claude sorts spending and gives kind, blunt student insights.

🍕

Club events

Tick your societies to see what's on, with free food flagged. Never miss a free pizza night.

🎉

Joyful and private

Tick tasks for confetti and a progress bar. No accounts: your data stays in your browser.
